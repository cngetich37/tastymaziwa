# **App Name**: TastyMaziwa Profit Tracker

## Core Features:

- Milk Delivery Input: Record daily milk delivery from the milkman in liters.
- Milk Sales Input: Record daily milk sales in liters and price per liter.
- Credit Sales Recording: Record milk sold on credit with expected payment date.
- Profit/Loss Calculation: Calculate daily, weekly, and monthly profit/loss based on delivery, sales, and credit data.
- Dashboard Overview: Display a summary dashboard showing key metrics like total milk delivered, sold, credit amount, and profit/loss.
- Amount Owed Recording: Record the amount owed to the milkman.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: HSL(48, 75%, 50%) - A golden yellow (#E6A720) to evoke the richness of milk and dairy.
- Background color: HSL(48, 20%, 95%) - A very light desaturated yellow (#F7F6F2) to provide a subtle, warm backdrop.
- Accent color: HSL(18, 75%, 50%) - A bright orange-red (#E65C20) to highlight important actions and data points.
- Body and headline font: 'PT Sans' (sans-serif) for a modern, readable interface.
- Use simple, line-based icons related to milk, sales, and finance for clarity.
- Clean and straightforward layout with clear sections for data input and reports.
- Subtle transitions and animations to enhance user experience without being distracting.
